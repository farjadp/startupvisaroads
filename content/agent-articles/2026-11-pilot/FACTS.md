## Official (IRCC / Canada Gazette)

- **19 Dec 2025 notice** (canada.ca, "immigration-measures-entrepreneurs"):
  - Start-up Visa optional work permit: no new applications from 19 Dec 2025,
    except extensions from people already in Canada holding an SUV work permit.
  - Start-up Visa PR: stops accepting new applications from 31 Dec 2025,
    11:59 p.m., except applicants holding a valid 2025 commitment certificate
    who had not yet applied.
  - Self-employed Persons Program: pause extended "until further notice".
  - "A new, targeted pilot for immigrant entrepreneurs is expected to follow";
    more information "will be communicated in 2026".
- **Start-up Visa Program page**: "paused on June 30, 2026"; applications
  accepted before the pause keep being processed; an open work permit can
  still be applied for if eligible. Paused to address backlog and wait times.
- **Deputy Minister transition binder 2026, economic immigration**: "launch of
  a new High Impact Start-up Pilot to replace the Start-Up Visa Program". No
  date, criteria or targets. 2026 admissions target for ALL federal business
  programs together: 500. IRCC is "still working on an inventory management
  strategy".
- **Assessing the application** (page dated 12 Mar 2026): intake paused as of
  1 Jan 2026 under new Ministerial Instructions; commitment certificates are
  valid six months, so some applications were accepted until 30 Jun 2026.
  Minimum committed capital: CAD 200,000 (venture fund), CAD 75,000 (angel
  group); incubators prioritised with CAD 75,000 committed. CLB 5 in all four
  skills, test under two years old. Each applicant at least 10% of voting
  rights; applicants plus the designated entity more than 50%. Equity only, no
  convertible debt.
- **Group-application cap** (IRCC, quoted in `lib/canada-suv.ts`): since 1 April
  2024, 10 complete group applications per designated organization per year.
- **Canada Gazette Part I, 20 Dec 2025**: index entry "New Ministerial
  Instructions" for the Start-up Business Class and Self-employed Persons
  Class. Title only; the text has not been read.

## Reported (not IRCC)

- **immigration.ca (24 Mar 2026)**, quoting IRCC's 2026-27 Departmental Plan:
  "new high impact Start-Up Visa pilot that will replace and address observed
  issues with the existing Start-Up Visa program"; focus on "elite
  entrepreneurs". Everything else in it is the author's inference.
- **immigration.ca admissions figures, source not named**: 55 (2015), 5,605
  (2024), 1,425 (2025).
- **Dec 2025 trade press reporting**: a cap of about 2,000 a year and a 12-month
  processing target. Not on canada.ca; always attributed as trade press reporting, never as a figure.
- **RBC, Sept 2026**: the programme granted PR on capital or incubator
  backing, "regardless of whether the business idea proved genuine or
  successful"; suggests Canada pilot a temporary entrepreneurship pathway.
  A recommendation, not policy.
