# Spain filing series: verified facts

Read 8 Nov 2026. Six pages, all official. Every number in the articles must
come from this file; `build.mjs` fails otherwise. Fee amounts are deliberately
absent: the form prints the amount and it changes yearly.

## Sede electrónica, Ministerio de Inclusión (procedure page)
https://sede.inclusion.gob.es/en/w/presentacion-solicitudes-autorizacion-residencia
- Procedure: "Presentation of applications for residence authorization for
  international mobility (Authorizations regulated by Law 14/2013)". SIA code
  202099. Managing unit: Subdirección General de Gestión y Coordinación de
  Flujos Migratorios (Dirección General de Migraciones).
- Legal basis listed: Ley 14/2013, Title V, Section Two, arts. 63 to 76, and
  Order PCM/1238/2021 publishing the Council of Ministers agreement of
  2 Nov 2021 with the procedure instructions.
- Fee: modelo 790, código 038, point 7 "Application for international mobility
  authorisations (Law 14/2013)", initial and renewed residence, holder and
  family. Paying yields the NRC (Número de Referencia Completo), needed to file.
- Forms: MI-T (holders), MI-T-Audiovisual (audiovisual sector), MI-F (family).
  A manual on validating signatures in PDFs is linked.
- Technical note: if access or signing fails, update Autofirma to version
  1.9.0, clear the cache; downloads at firmaelectronica.gob.es.
- The English version of the page is machine-translated (its own notice).

## UGE — Solicitudes
https://www.inclusion.gob.es/en/web/unidadgrandesempresas/solicitudes
- Applications under Ley 14/2013 and ALL related documents (corrections,
  additional documents, appeals, communications) must be filed electronically
  through the Ministry's sede electrónica. One application model for every
  Ley 14/2013 authorisation.
- Other models listed: affidavit of maintenance of family bond; responsible
  declaration of sufficiency of resources; two EU research / ICT displacement
  communication models.
- "To apply, you will need an electronic certificate." Links: information on
  digital certificates, help manuals, instructions for the entrepreneur
  authorisation, the tasa 790-038 form (point 7).

## Ministerio de Justicia — FNMT certificate by video identification
https://www.mjusticia.gob.es/es/certificados-digitales-clave/obtencion-certificado-video
- Four steps: prior configuration (install the FNMT configurator), request
  (enter DNI or NIE, first surname, email; a request code arrives by email;
  create a password that is asked again at download; a forgotten password
  means starting over), identity verification (request code + mobile number,
  SMS, scan a QR with the phone, show both sides of the ID document, video
  selfie with liveness check; FNMT reviews the evidence and emails when the
  certificate can be downloaded), download (same password; backup recommended).
- The request asks for a DNI or NIE: a person without either cannot use it.

## FNMT sede — Acreditar identidad (natural-person software certificate)
https://www.sede.fnmt.gob.es/certificados/persona-fisica/obtener-certificado-software/acreditar-identidad
- Four steps: configuration, request, identity accreditation, download. After
  accreditation at an office the download is available immediately.
- Only people aged 18 or over (or emancipated minors). Original, valid ID or
  passport, or an officially certified copy.
- Foreign citizen (non-EU): request code + Tarjeta de Identificación de
  Extranjero (red, green or white) showing the NIE, with passport; or the
  official NIF/NIE grant document, with passport.
- EU citizen: request code + NIE document / Certificado de Ciudadano de la
  Unión / NIE grant document, each with passport or national ID.
- More than 2,400 accreditation offices in Spain; office locator; AEAT,
  Social Security and other offices need an appointment. NOT at Correos.
- Abroad: Spain's career consular offices can accredit identity (not customs
  registries).
- If you cannot attend: a third person may go, but only with your signature
  on the contract legitimised before a notary; a power of attorney is not
  accepted.
- The digital DNI must be accepted by offices from 2 April 2026.

## BOE-A-2022-21739 — Ley 28/2022, de 21 de diciembre (Startups Law)
https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2022-21739
- Published BOE núm. 306, 22 Dec 2022. In force the day after publication
  (final provision 13). ELI: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2022/12/21/28
- Art. 3 "empresa emergente": a legal person meeting ALL of: newly created or
  no more than 5 years since registration (7 for biotech, energy, industrial
  and other strategic sectors or own technology designed in Spain); not born
  from a merger, spin-off or transformation of non-startups; has never paid
  dividends; not listed on a regulated market; registered office or permanent
  establishment in Spain; 60% of staff on employment contracts in Spain; an
  innovative project with a scalable business model (art. 4). Innovative =
  solves a problem or improves a situation through new or substantially
  improved products, services or processes, with a risk of technological,
  industrial or business-model failure.
- Art. 4: ENISA certifies the innovative and scalable character; decision
  within 3 months of a complete application; no answer = granted by positive
  silence; clock stops on requests for documents. Criteria: innovation (public
  funding in the last 3 years, R&D spend over the last 2 years or 1 for younger
  firms), market attractiveness, stage (prototype, MVP, market), scalability,
  competition, team, dependence on suppliers, customers; ENISA may refuse for
  reputational, regulatory, ethical or speculative doubts. ENISA publishes a
  procedure guide.
- Final provision 5 amends Ley 14/2013:
  - Art. 61.1 adds f) international teleworkers.
  - Art. 62.3 c): criminal record for the last 2 years of residence, plus a
    responsible declaration covering the last 5 years.
  - Art. 67.1: initial investor permit 3 years (was 2).
  - Art. 68 (one-year visa to prepare a business) is deleted.
  - Art. 69: entrepreneur permit valid 3 years; renewal 2 years; permanent
    residence possible at 5. Filed electronically with the UGE by the
    applicant or a legal representative. From abroad: one single application
    starts authorisation then visa, consecutively.
  - Art. 70: innovative AND/OR of special economic interest; favourable report
    by ENISA, requested ex officio by the UGE, mandatory, issued within 10
    working days. From abroad: visa applied for once the permit is granted.
    Assessment: applicant's profile and involvement (each partner assessed,
    visa or not); business plan with project, product/service and financing
    (investment required, possible sources); added value, innovation or
    investment opportunities.
  - Art. 71 highly qualified: now includes higher vocational graduates and
    specialists with 3 years' comparable experience; company registered with
    the UGE for 3 renewable years; permit 3 years, renewal 2, permanent at 5.
  - Art. 76.4 new: the passport is sufficient to register with Social Security
    during the first 6 months when the holder has no NIE yet.
  - New chapter V bis: international teleworking visa (max 1 year) and permit
    (max 3 years, renewals of 2).
  - DA 17: graduates (EQF level 6+) may stay up to 24 months to seek work or
    start a business.
  - DA 20: instructions with specific requirements due by 31 Mar 2023; means
    thresholds referenced to the SMI.
  - DA 21: NIE issuance to be facilitated for applicants under this law, with
    a dedicated web page for appointments.

## BOE-A-2013-10074 — Ley 14/2013, de 27 de septiembre (original text)
https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2013-10074
- Published BOE núm. 233, 28 Sep 2013. ELI: https://www.boe.es/eli/es/l/2013/09/27/14
- This is the ORIGINAL text. The consolidated text (act.php) is what applies
  today; the doc.php page shows what the law said before 2022.
- Original art. 61: investors, entrepreneurs, highly qualified, researchers,
  intra-company transferees; not EU citizens.
- Original art. 62.3: not irregular in Spain; 18 or over; no criminal record in
  Spain or countries of residence in the last 5 years; not refused entry in the
  Schengen area; public or private health insurance with an insurer authorised
  in Spain; sufficient means for self and family; fee paid. 62.4: spouse and
  children under 18 (or dependent adult children) may apply jointly.
- Original art. 67: investor permit 2 years, renewals of 2.
- Original art. 68: a one-year visa to carry out preparatory steps for an
  entrepreneurial activity (deleted in 2022).
- Original art. 69: entrepreneur permit, national validity; no fixed duration
  in the article itself.
- Original art. 70: innovative WITH special economic interest; favourable
  report from "the competent body of the central administration"; job creation
  in Spain weighed first; also profile, business plan (market, product,
  financing), added value.
- Original art. 75.4/75.5: residence visas valid 1 year without a TIE; visa
  decided and notified within 10 working days except Visa Code art. 22
  consultation cases.
- Original art. 76: processed by the UGE by electronic means, granted by the
  Dirección General de Migraciones; 20 days to decide; silence = granted;
  reasoned decisions; recurso de alzada; renewals of 2 years.
