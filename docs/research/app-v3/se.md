### "2024–2025 rule changes" (asked by the lead)
No change aimed at self-employed people in 2024 or 2025 was found on migrationsverket.se. What does apply: PR conditions must hold both at application and at decision (described on the extension page); stricter good-conduct rules for all residence applicants from 13 Jul 2026. The 2024–2025 tightening (work-permit salary floor raised step by step to 80% of median; track change abolished 1 Apr 2025) and the 1 Jun 2026 rules (90% of median = SEK 34,470/month from 16 Jun 2026, health insurance) concern employees. EU Blue Card rules changed in January 2025 (news page found in search, not opened). Treat this as "not found", not as "no change".
