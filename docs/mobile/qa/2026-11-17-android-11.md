## Findings
1. Results card has a flat dark-grey block (about 1000x240 px) above the first route (Austria) on the results screen. It persisted 5+ s with network up (ping OK). It looks like an image or hero that failed to render. Not reproduced on the second card. Evidence: results-01-en-android-light.png, results-02-en-android-light.png. Unknown whether it is an emulator artefact. Needs a look on a real device or the iOS build.
2. Route header for an Austrian route shows the eyebrow label "EXPRESS ENTRY" (route-01-en-android-light.png). Express Entry is Canada's system, so it is likely a wrong family label, though it may be the family name by design. Product to confirm.
3. Assessment picker: after choosing an option, the first tap on "Done" was dropped several times (sheet stayed open, needed a second tap). Possibly an adb timing artefact (my taps were scripted about 1 s after the selection); needs a human check. Minor copy/typography: 'Every question has "Prefer not to say"' uses a closing quote glyph at the start. Also the assessment shows "16" questions where the brief says 10 (8 per screen); confirm whether the brief or the app is right.
Also noted: results show "100%" together with "based on 4 of 5 conditions" / "2 of 4 conditions" and a "NOT CHECKED HERE" list; the match-trust rules (coverage shown) appear to be followed, but I did not check them against the spec.
