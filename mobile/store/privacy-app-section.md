## The VisaRoads mobile app

This section applies to the VisaRoads app for iPhone and Android, published by
Ashavid Inc.

**What the app collects:** nothing. The app has no account, and no sign-up or
sign-in. It does not include analytics, advertising or crash-reporting
software. What you enter — your pathfinder answers, the steps you mark done,
your language and appearance settings — is stored only on your phone and
never sent to us or to anyone else.

**What the app downloads:** to show the News tab, the app requests the public
article feeds of visaroads.com. The request carries no personal information.
Like any web server, ours may record the technical details of a request (such
as an IP address) in standard server logs. These are not linked to you and
are not used to identify you.

**Links that leave the app:** official sources, articles, photo credits and
"Book a call" open web pages in your phone's browser view. "Message on
Telegram" opens Telegram. Those pages and services have their own privacy
policies. If you contact us through them, the rest of this policy covers what
you send.

**Deleting your data:** in the app, go to Me › Settings › Delete my data to
erase your answers, steps and progress (your language and appearance choices
are kept). Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored.

**Children:** the app is intended for adults planning business immigration and
is not directed to children.

**Changes:** if a future version of the app starts collecting any data, we will
update this section, and the store listings, before that version is released.
