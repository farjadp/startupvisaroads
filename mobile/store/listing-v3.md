TWO WAYS IN
• Choose a road: pick a country, then a route. • Get assessed: ten short questions on two screens, each with "Prefer not to say", no sign-up. Your routes come back ranked by how closely you match their published criteria.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 68 routes in 17 countries: Canada, the United States, Australia, Denmark, Finland, Estonia, the Netherlands, Italy, Spain, Austria, Ireland, Norway, Sweden, Bulgaria, Türkiye, Israel and the UAE.
• Five families of routes: startup and innovation visas; entrepreneur streams, including Canada's provincial nominee programs; talent routes such as EB-2 NIW, EB-1A, O-1A and the EU Blue Card; study permits; and skilled-worker routes such as Express Entry.

• Express Entry: a calculator on IRCC's grid with a live score, what would add the most points, a converter for IELTS, CELPIP, TEF and TCF results, the latest rounds of invitations, and a check of whether your occupation is on the category-draw lists (our occupation list is partial).
